Code of Conduct

The short version: treat people with respect. The full version is below.

What we expect

Everyone at Triangle AI Hackathon, participants, mentors, judges, sponsors, and organizers, is expected to treat each other with respect for the full weekend, including in any online spaces connected to the event before or after.

What counts as harassment

Harassment includes offensive comments related to gender, age, sexual orientation, disability, appearance, body size, race, religion, or economic status, along with intimidation, stalking, unwanted photography or recording, disrupting talks or sessions, and unwanted physical contact.

What happens if this policy is broken

Organizers can remove anyone who violates this policy from the event at their discretion, regardless of registration status.

How to report something

If you are being harassed or see someone else being harassed, tell an organizer right away, either in person at the help desk or by emailing triangleaihackathon@duke.edu.
